Greta Thunberg is a Swedish climate activist who became internationally known for campaigning against climate change and demanding stronger action from governments.

 Why isn't she verified on TikTok? 

Greta Thunberg has been **nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize multiple times**:

* **2019**

   — She was nominated by Norwegian MPs. She was 

  **16 years old**

   at the time, making her one of the youngest-ever nominees.
* **2020**

   — She was again nominated, this time by a group of Swedish lawmakers.
* **2021**

   — She was nominated again by members of the Swedish parliament.
* **2022**

   — She was among people nominated by Norwegian parliamentarians

TikTok says verification is not automatic just because someone is famous. It says it considers factors such as whether the account is "authentic, unique, active, notable, and follows its rules". 

The deeper problem: **TikTok's verification and enforcement systems are separate questions.** The fact that TikTok hasn't removed an account doesn't establish that TikTok has authenticated it. Likewise, an account being unverified doesn't establish that it's fake.

An account can accumulate millions of followers while still being impersonating, unofficial, fan-run, or simply ambiguously affiliated.

**Fast Facts About Greta Thunberg**

\- Born: 3 January 2003, in Stockholm, Sweden.

\- Nationality: Swedish.

\- Known for: Climate activism and the Fridays for Future movement.

\- Became prominent: In 2018, when she began skipping school on Fridays to protest outside the Swedish Parliament, demanding stronger climate action.

\- International impact: Inspired millions of young people worldwide to participate in climate strikes.

\- Famous speech: Addressed world leaders at the 2019 United Nations Climate Action Summit, criticizing their inadequate response to the climate crisis.

 Greta continues to be active in political and environmental causes beyond climate change. In September 2026, Swedish broadcaster SVT reported on her continued activism and the threats she says she receives because of her work.
