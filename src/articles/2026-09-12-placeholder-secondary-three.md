# **Ms Rachel Calls Out 'Uncancellable' Celebrities Over Gaza Silence**

**Source:** [@msrachelforlittles](https://www.tiktok.com/@msrachelforlittles) via TikTok | **Guest Appearance:** [@itsopen.podcasts](https://www.tiktok.com/@itsopen.podcasts)
**Category:** Media & Culture / Current Affairs
**Engagement Metrics:** 112.1K Likes | 1,119 Comments | 3,656 Saves | 2,174 Shares

In a viral TikTok clip, popular children's educator Ms Rachel addresses celebrity silence surrounding the humanitarian crisis in Gaza during an appearance on *@itsopen.podcasts*. 

Dressed in a light blue sleeveless top and pink scrunchie while speaking into a podcast microphone, she directly challenges high-profile figures who hold "uncancellable" status to leverage their platforms to protect civilian lives, schools, and hospitals.

> *"If you are so famous and you're uncancellable... why can't you risk something to help save lives?"*
>
> *"It's so obviously a genocide and it's important to say genocide right now because saying it will help move the world hopefully to stop it."*
>
> *"Have to be like you can't bully us into not saying people deserve to live and don't deserve to be bombed."*

The video highlights an ongoing debate regarding the responsibility of digital creators and public figures to engage with global political issues.
